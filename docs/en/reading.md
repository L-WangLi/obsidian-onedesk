# Reading

Stats and your latest thoughts from book notes.

![Reading](../screenshots/05-reading.webp)

## Which notes are read

Every note in `Shelf/` is a book, except files with *dashboard* or *readme* in the name. The format follows common reading-note exporters, such as WeChat Reading exports:

```markdown
---
progress: 62
readingTime: 5小时20分钟
readingDate: 2026-08-15
---
> - 分类： Personal growth-Learning

> 📌 a highlighted passage
- 💭 your thought
- ⏱ 2026-09-12 21:35:40
```

- `📌` marks a highlight, `💭` a thought, `⏱` its time.
- `progress: 100` means finished; `readingTime` feeds the hours.

## The page

| Stat | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Books** | Number of books |
| **Hours** | Total reading time |
| **Thoughts** | Thoughts written (💭) |
| **Highlights** | Highlights (📌) |
| **Finished** | Books finished, and the share of all books |
| **Ratio** | Highlights per thought; lower means more active reading |

**Recent** lists the 5 or 10 books you most recently wrote thoughts about, each with the passage, your thought and when. **Open library** opens `Shelf/`.
