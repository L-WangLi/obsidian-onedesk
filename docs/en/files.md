# Files

Browse, preview and edit your vault without leaving OneDesk.

![Files](../screenshots/03-files.webp)

- **Left**: top-level folders ordered by recent activity, with note counts and last-modified times, under a pinned *Recent*. Folders untouched for a long while are tucked below.
- **Middle**: notes and subfolders in the selection, newest first. Step into subfolders and back out with the breadcrumb.
- **Right**: a rendered preview of the selected note. Switch to editing to change it in place; edits are written back to the file. If the note changed elsewhere after you opened it — synced from another device, say — your save won't overwrite that change.
- **Search**: the box at the top matches **file names** across the whole vault; the total note count sits beside it.
- **+ Note**: a blank, date-named note in the folder the middle column is showing — or in the inbox, when that is *Recent* or search results.
